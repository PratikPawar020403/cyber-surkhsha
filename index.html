Critical Advisory

'Digital Arrest' & Police Video Call Extortion

Fraudsters wear fake police uniforms on Skype, display forged Supreme Court/CBI warrants, and claim your Aadhaar is linked to narcotic parcels in Mumbai.

  • The Law: No law enforcement agency conducts trials or arrests over video calls.
  • Never Pay: There is no such thing as an "RBI Verification Escrow Account".
  • Immediate Step: Hang up immediately and file a report on this portal.
MHA Advisory #2024/09
Financial Defense

UPI QR Code & 'Collect Request' Trap

Scammers impersonate buyers on OLX or customer support agents sending you a QR code claiming 'Scan to receive your refund/cashback'.

  • Golden Rule: Entering your UPI PIN ALWAYS debits money from your account.
  • No PIN for Receiving: Receiving money requires only your phone number or UPI ID.
  • Lien Action: If money is lost, call 1930 within the Golden Hour.
NPCI Security Directive
Device Threat

Electricity Bill & Courier .APK Malware

Urgent SMS messages warning that your electricity will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM, instructing you to install an APK file from a link.

  • Remote Trojan: The APK intercepts and forwards banking SMS and OTPs silently.
  • Immediate Fix: Turn on Airplane Mode to stop data transfer, then uninstall.
  • Bank Alert: Temporarily freeze netbanking credentials from another phone.
CERT-In Vulnerability Note
Employment Scam

Telegram Part-Time Task & Crypto Scam

Offers paying ₹500/hour for liking YouTube videos or reviewing Google Maps hotels, later demanding deposit 'investments' to withdraw earnings.

  • Psychological Hook: Small initial payments (₹150–₹300) build false trust.
  • The Reality: Funds in the "crypto dashboard" are simulated numbers.
  • Protection: Real employers never ask you to pay money to get paid.
I4C Intelligence Alert

Video Blackmail & AI Morphed Photos

Criminal syndicates use recorded video call snippets or AI face-swapped media to blackmail victims for continuous ransom payments.

  • Do NOT Pay: Paying ransom never deletes media; it only escalates demands.
  • Anonymous Path: Use our confidential anonymous reporting pathway to protect identity.
  • Legal Backing: Penalized under IT Act Sec 66E, 67, 67A with up to 5 yrs imprisonment.

Telecom Security

SIM Swap & Unauthorized eSIM Porting

Fraudsters trick telecom customer care into issuing a duplicate SIM or eSIM QR code, disabling your phone while intercepting your banking OTPs.

  • Early Warning: Sudden "No Service" or network disconnection on your primary phone.
  • Action: Immediately call your telecom provider from another phone to block the SIM.
  • Bank Alert: Inform your bank immediately to freeze SMS-based transaction channels.
